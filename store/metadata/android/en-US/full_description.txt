CaptureCam is a simple, free screen recorder with no ads, no accounts and no internet access. Your recordings never leave your phone.

RECORD WHAT YOU WANT
• Record your whole screen or just one app
• Pause and resume at any time
• Optional on-screen 3-2-1 countdown, so you can get ready
• Quick Settings tile to start and stop without opening the app
• Controls right in the notification

YOUR VIDEOS, YOUR WAY
• Choose resolution (up to your screen's native size), frames per second and quality
• Portrait, landscape or automatic orientation
• Records app sound (on by default) and, optionally, the microphone
• Saved as standard MP4 files in Movies or Downloads, ready to share

BUILT-IN GALLERY
• Browse your recent recordings in a clean grid
• Preview them inside the app
• Share, open with another app or delete in a tap

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads and no trackers
• The app doesn't even have the internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere
• Free and open source

GOOD TO KNOW
• Requires Android 11 or newer
• App sound depends on each app: apps that block audio capture, and protected content, are recorded without sound
• Apps that block screen capture (some banking or video apps) will appear black
• The optional "Display over other apps" permission is only used to show the on-screen countdown

Questions or ideas? Find the source code and contact details inside the app, under "About CaptureCam".
